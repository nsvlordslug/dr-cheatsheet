Node Structure

▼

Standard Footage

01
Color Correction
Lift, Gamma, Gain, Temp, Contrast. Fix exposure first before anything else.
02
LUT
Apply LUT here. Dial back Key Output Gain — don't crush the image.
03
Final Tweaks
Saturation, small adjustments. Often left untouched.

iLog Footage (Ace Pro 2)

01
Subtle Exposure
Light correction before LUT. iLog is flat — don't go heavy here.
02
LUT
Insta360 official LUT or Ben Claremont LUTs.
03
Final Tweaks
Usually minimal — iLog LUTs are well-balanced already.
🔵

Color Wheels

▼
Lift
Shadows — darkest parts of your image.
Left = crush blacks · Right = matte
Gamma
Midtones — between shadows and highlights.
Most visible brightness change
Gain
Highlights — the brightest parts.
Watch waveform — don't clip 1023
Offset
Moves everything at once — all tones together.
Quick overall exposure shifts

Temp = warm/cool · Contrast = darks vs brights · Saturation = color intensity. Don't chase numbers — chase the image.

📊

Scopes

▼
Waveform
Shows brightness left→right of your image. Bottom = shadows, top = highlights.
Nothing crushed at 0. Nothing clipping at 1023.
Vectorscope
Shows color — hue and saturation in a circle. Center = no color. Further out = more saturated. Skin tones follow the 11 o'clock line.

Noise Reduction

▼
1
Open Mixer Panel
Find the NR button on the track. Click to open settings.
2
Find a silent moment
Playhead on background noise only — no talking, no action.
3
Learn noise profile
Hit Learn. Resolve samples that silence as the noise floor.
4
Apply and check
Don't over-reduce — audio can go robotic.

Different environments = separate tracks, separate noise profiles.

EQ Tips

▼
240 HzHollow/boomy bass — cut here
2–4 kHzHarsh vocals — sweep to find and cut
6 kHzHarsh highs — cut slightly

Sweep technique: Boost a band high, sweep slowly until it sounds worst, then cut that frequency.

Things to Remember

▼

Grab a still before risky operations. Saved a grade when Apply Grade wiped node work.

HoverAdjust is always listening. If a clip is selected and inspector is open, the Knob will move sliders. Know what's selected.

Apply Grade from Gallery is the preferred method for copying grades between clips — not copy/paste.

Don't chase numbers, chase the image. Scopes are guides, not rules.

Medal grain at 720p was low bitrate. Now 1080p/60fps/50M/H.264 — future clips will be clean.
